  - Panel talk focusing on cyber career specializations: SOC Analyst, Penetration Tester, Incident Responder, Threat Hunter.
  - Practical roadmap for cracking Big 4 (Deloitte, KPMG) and tech enterprise security interviews.
  - Importance of hands-on platforms (TryHackMe, HackTheBox) and global certifications (CEH, Security+, OSCP).
* **Day 1 Afternoon (01:00 PM – 04:00 PM) — Email & Image Forensics:**
  - **Email Protocol Integrity:** Theoretical and header inspection of SPF (Sender Policy Framework), DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail), and DMARC.
  - **Raw Header Analysis:** Extracting sending hops, return paths, and originating IPs via MX Toolbox and DNS reverse lookups.
  - **Sender Reputation:** Checking IP blacklisting via `multirbl.valli.org`.
  - **EXIF Metadata Forensics:** Extracting camera hardware details, GPS coordinates, timestamps, and editing tool signatures via EXIF tools.
  - **Photo Tampering Detection:** Utilizing Forensically (`29a.ch/photo-forensics`) for Magnification, Clone Detection, and Error Level Analysis (ELA) to detect JPEG resaving artifacts.
  - **KC7 Forensic Challenge:** Fast-paced gamified forensic challenge reinforcing learned skills.
* **Day 2 Full Day (09:30 AM – 04:00 PM) — Advanced Forensic Drills with Autopsy:**
  - **Evidence Integrity & Chain of Custody:** Standards for acquisition, hashing (MD5, SHA-256), and preservation under legal frameworks.
  - **Autopsy Case Configuration:** Initializing investigative cases, mounting logical files and raw/E01 forensic disk images.
  - **Carving & Deleted File Recovery:** Master File Table (MFT) structures, slack space analysis, and recovering deleted images/documents.
  - **Artifact Indexing:** Global keyword search, web browser history analysis, registry triage, and USB device tracking.
  - **Valedictory & Prize Distribution:** Recognition of high-scoring investigative teams in the KC7 challenge.
