## 7. Summary of the Event (Day-wise Technical Breakdown)

### 7.1. Day 1: Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) & Future-Proof Security (09:30 AM – 04:00 PM)
* **Inaugural Formalities:** Welcome address by student coordinators Khyathi Mallula and Koushik Ram Reddy, followed by address by Faculty Coordinators Ms. Kavita Agarwal and Ms. Puvvula Kiranmaie.
* **Classical Cryptography Foundations:** Working principles of symmetric (AES) vs asymmetric algorithms (RSA, ECC, Diffie-Hellman) and digital signature algorithms (ECDSA).
* **The Quantum Threat Surface:** Theoretical review of quantum computational power, Shor’s Algorithm (exponential speedup in integer factorization and discrete logarithms), and Grover’s Algorithm (quadratic speedup on symmetric key searches).
* **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC):** Deep-dive into NIST PQC standardization:
  - Lattice-based cryptography (CRYSTALS-Kyber, CRYSTALS-Dilithium).
  - Code-based and hash-based signature schemes (SPHINCS+).
  - Practical enterprise strategies for crypto-agility and quantum-resilient infrastructure.
* **Felicitation:** Chief Guest Chanukya Potnuru was felicitated with a traditional shawl and memento by the faculty conveners.

### 7.2. Day 2: Ethical Hacking & Blockchain Architecture (09:30 AM – 04:00 PM)
* **Morning Track — Ethical Hacking & Bug Bounties (09:30 AM – 12:30 PM):**
  - Led by Dhruv Gupta (DDC Alumnus) and Rajesh Sagar (KPMG).
  - Practical bug hunting methodology, recon frameworks, parameter tampering, and OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities.
  - Hands-on exercises on identifying weak password patterns, credential stuffing, and controlled password cracking.
  - Ethical guidelines, responsible disclosure programs (HackerOne, Bugcrowd), and defensive hardening.
* **Afternoon Track — Blockchain Technology & Security (01:30 PM – 03:30 PM):**
  - Led by Dr. VamsiNath Javangula (ICFAI University).
  - Fundamentals of distributed ledgers, peer-to-peer networks, cryptographic hashing, and Byzantine Fault Tolerance.
  - Transaction lifecycle, consensus mechanisms (Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake), and immutability.
  - Smart contract security vulnerabilities (reentrancy attacks, integer overflow, flash loan exploits).
* **Valedictory & Felicitations (03:30 PM – 04:00 PM):**
  - Formal felicitation of Dr. VamsiNath Javangula by faculty coordinators.
  - Participant feedback collection and distribution of certificates.
