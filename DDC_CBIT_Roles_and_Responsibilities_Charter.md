## 1. Club Foundation, Vision & Strategic Objectives

The **Digital Defence Club (DDC)** is the premier student-driven cybersecurity and technology organization of Chaitanya Bharathi Institute of Technology (CBIT), Hyderabad. Founded under the joint academic guidance of the **Department of Computer Engineering and Technology (CET)**, the **Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI&DS)**, and the **Department of Information Technology (IT)**, the club is dedicated to cultivating high-caliber technical competency in offensive security, defensive engineering, digital forensics, post-quantum cryptography, AI/LLM security, and blockchain technologies.

### 1.1 Core Mission Pillars
* **Technical Rigor & Practical Learning:** Empowering students with cutting-edge, hands-on training in vulnerability assessment, digital forensics, reverse engineering, and threat intelligence.
* **National-Scale Event Execution:** Planning and executing premier technical initiatives including 24h/36h Hackathons (CyberFest), Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges (CryptX), and specialized workshops (FornensIQ, QuantumSecure).
* **Industry & Government Collaboration:** Connecting students with top-tier cybersecurity enterprises, government bodies (DSCI, CCoE), and global certifications (CEH, CISSP, SANS).
* **Ethical Security & Community Defense:** Fostering strict white-hat ethics, legal compliance under the Information Technology Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, and responsible disclosure.

## 3. Detailed Roles & Responsibilities Matrix

### 3.1 Institutional Patrons & Faculty Coordinators
* **Institutional Compliance & Approvals:** Provide institutional authorization, venue approvals, lab reservations, and administrative alignment across CET, AI&DS, IT, and CBIT academic schedules.
* **Financial & Budgetary Endorsement:** Review and endorse budget estimations, fund requisition letters, and post-event audited financial statements submitted to the Principal.
* **External Liaison:** Act as the primary institutional link between DDC, college administration, external government entities (DSCI, CCoE), and corporate sponsors.
* **Quality Assurance:** Review and approve all official event reports, press releases, and IQAC documentation to maintain accredited quality standards.

### 3.2 President (Core Committee)
*The President is the executive head and principal representative of the Digital Defence Club, holding ultimate accountability for strategic direction and institutional delivery.*
* **Strategic Vision:** Define the annual roadmap, strategic objectives, and event calendar for the club.
* **Institutional Representation:** Represent DDC before the Principal, HoDs, Faculty Coordinators, and external dignitaries/sponsors.
* **Executive Supervision:** Supervise all Executive Board (EB) wings, ensuring timely milestone completion and cross-functional harmony.
* **Conflict Resolution:** Mediate internal escalations, resolve bottlenecks, and ensure high operational discipline across the organization.
* **Governance & Sign-off:** Review and co-sign all major budget requests, permission letters, and official event reports.

### 3.3 Vice President (Core Committee)
*The Vice President acts as the chief operating officer of DDC, translating strategic mandates into ground execution.*
* **Operations Oversight:** Directly oversee the Heads of Technical, Events, PR, Design, and Documentation teams.
* **Risk Management & Contingencies:** Develop contingency protocols for power outages, network failures, speaker delays, and logistical emergencies during national conclaves.
* **Executive Proxy:** Serve as the primary deputy to the President, assuming full executive responsibilities in the President's absence.
* **Team Performance:** Ensure high volunteer motivation, task balancing, and efficient workload distribution.

### 3.4 General Secretary (Core Committee)
*The General Secretary serves as the administrative custodian and official communicator of the club.*
* **Administrative Coordination:** Schedule core committee and executive board meetings, draft agendas, and record Minutes of Meetings (MoM).
* **Official Communications:** Author and track official correspondence, departmental permission letters, circulars, and participant notifications.
* **Records Custody:** Maintain central registers of registered members, event attendance, and core committee action items.
* **Execution Tracking:** Ensure strict timeline adherence across all departments for pre-event deliverables.

### 3.5 Joint Secretaries (Core Committee — 2 Positions)
* **Volunteer Mobilization:** Manage on-ground volunteer deployment, shifts, and task allocation during major events.
* **Attendance & On-Ground Coordination:** Supervise attendance desks, verification lines, and participant query resolution.
* **Grievance Redressal:** Address grievances from student participants and junior club members swiftly.
* **Inter-Departmental Link:** Coordinate with individual class representatives and student clubs across CBIT.

### 3.6 Treasurer (Core Committee)
*The Treasurer holds singular responsibility for financial integrity, budget estimation, fund requisition, and expense auditing.*
* **Budget Formulation:** Prepare detailed itemized budget estimates for every workshop, hackathon, and guest talk in consultation with the President and Faculty Coordinators.
* **Fund Management & Disbursements:** Track and disburse funds for banners, mementos, food, kits, prize money, and technical equipment.
* **Invoice & Receipt Auditing:** Collect, verify, and preserve all original GST invoices, payment receipts, and vouchers.
* **Financial Statements & Closure:** Compile the final post-event Financial Statement and submit it to the Accounts Department and Principal's Office within 5 working days.
* **Sponsorship Tracking:** Track sponsorship cash inflows and ensure timely allocation to respective prize pools and vendor payments.

## 6. Code of Conduct, Ethical Standards & Disciplinary Framework

> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY ETHICAL CODE**
> 1. **Strictly White-Hat:** All hacking tools, techniques, and methodologies taught or practiced under DDC must be utilized exclusively in authorized lab environments and sandboxes. Any unauthorized penetration testing, network sniffing, or tampering with CBIT IT infrastructure will result in immediate expulsion and disciplinary referral to the Principal.
> 2. **Professional Representation:** All DDC executives and members must exhibit exemplary decorum, respect, and hospitality toward visiting industry leaders, faculty, and inter-state student delegates.
> 3. **Equal Opportunity & Inclusivity:** DDC maintains zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any form based on gender, branch, year, or background.
> 4. **Overnight Security Protocol:** During 24h/36h national conclaves, participants and organizers must strictly adhere to campus boundary rules. Leaving campus premises during night hours without prior written consent from faculty coordinators is strictly prohibited.

## 7. Performance Evaluation, Tenure & Succession Protocol

* **Tenure Duration:** All Core Committee and Executive Board appointments are held for a tenure of one academic year (subject to semester performance audits).
* **Performance Benchmarks:** Active participation in mandatory meetings, contribution to at least 2 major events per semester, and strict adherence to assigned deadlines.
* **Certificate Issuance Criteria:** Only members who demonstrate exceptional dedication and fulfill their assigned charter responsibilities will receive the official Certificate of Leadership / Merit signed by HoDs and Principal.
* **Succession & Knowledge Transfer:** Outgoing Core Committee members are required to conduct formal transition sessions, transfer administrative Google Drives, and mentor newly inducted team heads.
