## 7. Summary of the Event (Day-wise Technical Breakdown)

### 7.1. Day 1 Morning: Cybersecurity Career & Placement Insights (09:30 AM – 12:00 PM)
* The morning session focused on career readiness, demystifying enterprise hiring processes, and technical screening in cybersecurity consulting firms.
* The five alumni speakers shared real-world interview experiences, case interviews, and behavioral assessments.
* Detailed discussions covered required technical proficiencies: network protocols, Linux administration, Python scripting for automation, and SOC tooling.
* Emphasis was placed on competitive credentials including CEH, CompTIA Security+, and practical CTF participation.

### 7.2. Day 1 Afternoon: Hands-on Email & Image Forensics (01:00 PM – 04:00 PM)
* **Digital Forensics Principles:** Chain of custody, legal evidence admissibility, hashing standards, and write-blocking principles.
* **Email Forensics & Header Triage:**
  - Deconstructing RFC 822/5322 raw internet headers.
  - Analyzing SPF, DKIM cryptographic signatures, and DMARC alignment policies to detect domain spoofing and phishing attacks.
  - Using online analyzers (MX Toolbox) and sender IP reputation databases (`multirbl.valli.org`).
* **Image Forensics & Metadata Analysis:**
  - Uncovering EXIF metadata (camera hardware, GPS coordinates, timestamps, software version).
  - Visual tampering inspection using Forensically (`29a.ch/photo-forensics`): Magnification, Clone Detection, and Error Level Analysis (ELA) to identify image splicing and digital alterations.
* **KC7 Challenge:** Gamified investigative puzzle requiring attendees to triage email compromises and tampered photographic evidence.

### 7.3. Day 2: Advanced Forensic Investigations with Autopsy (09:30 AM – 04:00 PM)
* **Forensic Environment Setup:** Case initialization, investigator tagging, and creating SQLite local forensic databases.
* **Evidence Ingestion:** Mounting raw disk images (DD) and EnCase evidence files (E01) without altering evidence integrity.
* **File System Analysis & Data Carving:** Recovering deleted artifacts from NTFS Master File Table (MFT) and unallocated disk clusters.
* **Keyword Indexing & Activity Triage:** RegEx pattern searching for credit card numbers and passwords, analyzing browser history/cache, and inspecting Windows Event Logs.
* **Valedictory Ceremony:** High-performing teams were recognized with certificates of merit, followed by closing remarks by Faculty Coordinators.
