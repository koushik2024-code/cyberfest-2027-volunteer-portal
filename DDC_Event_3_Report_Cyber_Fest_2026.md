## 7. Summary of the Event (36-Hour Continuous Execution)

### 7.1. Day 1: 6th February 2026
* **08:30 AM – 09:45 AM (Registration & Check-In):** Registration kit distribution, credential validation, and Ethernet LAN setup for 125+ teams.
* **09:45 AM – 11:00 AM (Grand Inaugural Ceremony):**
  - Lighting of the lamp and welcome addresses by Principal Dr. C. V. Narasimhulu and HoDs.
  - Inspiring keynote by Dr. Sriram Birudavolu outlining India's vision for becoming a global cybersecurity hub.
  - Address by Sri G. Vimal Kumar on industry expectations from emerging engineering graduates.
* **11:00 AM – 12:00 PM (Masterclass by Hacker School):** Exploitation methodology, reconnaissance, and web architecture vulnerabilities.
* **01:30 PM – 03:00 PM (Parallel Workshops):**
  - *Workshop 1: Offensive Security Toolkit* — Hands-on vulnerability enumeration, automated scanning, and exploit chaining.
  - *Workshop 2: Incident Response Drill* — Real-time SOC simulation analyzing log telemetry and mitigating intrusion vectors.
* **03:00 PM Onwards (The 36-Hour Hackathon & CTF Begins):**
  - *Track 1 (Hackathon):* Developing functional prototypes under the themes "AI in Cybersecurity" and "Blockchain Security".
  - *Track 2 (Capture The Flag):* Solving complex jeopardy-style challenges in Web Exploitation, Reverse Engineering, Cryptography, DFIR, and Binary Exploitation.
* **Overnight (Midnight Checkpoint Reviews):** Dedicated mentoring rounds, technical debugging, and midnight refreshments for participants remaining on campus.

### 7.2. Day 2: 7th February 2026
* **09:00 AM:** Final code push, deployment verification, and CTF submission freeze.
* **10:30 AM – 01:00 PM (Round 1 Project Judging):** 125 teams evaluated across 6 judging panels on technical complexity, novelty, defense architecture, and viability.
* **02:00 PM – 03:00 PM (Final Pitch Round):** Top 10 shortlisted teams presented live demonstrations to the executive jury in Assembly Hall.
* **03:00 PM – 04:00 PM (Grand Valedictory & Prize Felicitations):** Announcement of winners, presentation of cash prizes totaling ₹1,00,000, and institutional closing remarks.
