## 7. Summary of the Event (Session-wise Technical Breakdown)

### 7.1. Inaugural Ceremony & DDC Logo Unveiling (09:30 AM – 10:20 AM)
* The program commenced with the traditional lighting of the lamp by the Chief Guest, Directors, HoDs, and Faculty Coordinators, followed by an invocation song.
* Faculty Coordinators Ms. Kavita Agarwal (CET) and Ms. Puvvula Kiranmaie (AI&DS) welcomed the gathering and introduced the vision behind establishing a specialized cybersecurity club at CBIT.
* Prof. B. Linga Reddy emphasized the growing importance of ethical digital citizenship, while Prof. Prabhakar Reddy urged students to bridge academic theory with real-world security challenges.
* The official **Logo and Charter of the Digital Defence Club** were formally unveiled on stage.
* Club President Majji Pradeep Kumar delivered an inspiring presidential address, urging members to act as proactive "Defenders against Cybercrimes" and outlining the upcoming calendar of workshops and hackathons.

### 7.2. Technical Session: Cybersecurity Uncovered (10:20 AM – 11:45 AM)
The technical keynote led by Mr. Razi Ansari covered end-to-end security fundamentals:
1. **Developing the Hacker Mindset:** Emphasized disciplined, analytical problem-solving, continuous learning, and eliminating social media distractions to build deep domain expertise.
2. **Foundational Security & The CIA Triad:** Illustrated Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability using enterprise incident examples and architectural defense-in-depth principles.
3. **Attack Evolution & Case Studies:** Analyzed the WannaCry ransomware outbreak, detailing how weaponized NSA exploits (EternalBlue) caused global supply chain disruptions.
4. **Hands-on Vulnerability Identification:** Demonstrated practical reconnaissance and parameter fuzzing techniques using Burp Suite for web application testing.
5. **Career Vectors in Cyber Defense:** Outlined core pathways in Application Security (AppSec), Network Security, Cloud Governance, and Security Operations Center (SOC) engineering.

### 7.3. Interactive Q&A and Felicitation (11:45 AM – 12:00 PM)
* Students engaged in a lively Q&A session covering bug bounty platforms, responsible disclosure, and foundational certifications (CompTIA Security+, CEH).
* The session concluded with the formal felicitation of Mr. Razi Ansari by the faculty conveners and a vote of thanks by Vice President Vasanthadithya Mundrathi.
